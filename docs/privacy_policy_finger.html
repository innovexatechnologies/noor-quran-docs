Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Finger Pickora does not require an account. The app keeps settings and game history on your device and does not send them to a server.

This Privacy Policy explains how Finger Pickora ("the app") handles information when you use it.

Information the app uses

  • Settings: preferences such as theme, color palette, sound, and haptic feedback.
  • Game history: locally saved game type, date and time, result summary (which may include names entered for a team), and, where applicable, player count, winner name, or game duration.
  • Touch input: finger positions are processed temporarily during a Finger Picker round to choose a participant. The touch positions themselves are not saved as game history.

How information is stored and shared

Settings and game history are stored in the app's local device storage. The app does not operate an account system or send this information to the app developer. The app does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking services.

Device backups or operating-system services may handle app data according to your device settings and the platform provider's own policies. The app does not control those services.

Your choices

You can clear game history using the history controls in the app. You can change or reset app preferences in Settings. Removing the app or clearing its storage may delete locally stored information, subject to any device backup or restore settings.

Children's privacy

The app does not ask users to provide personal information or create an account. Game results or names that you choose to enter are stored locally on your device.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. The latest version and its update date will be published with the app.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the app publisher using the support contact provided on the app's official store listing.
