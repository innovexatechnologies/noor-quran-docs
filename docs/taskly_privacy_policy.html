Official Privacy Policy

Taskly - Task Manager

Developed by Innovexa Technologies • Package: com.innovexa.taskly
Effective Date: November 19, 2026 | Last Updated: November 19, 2026

🔒 Summary: Taskly is a privacy-first, offline task manager. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information or task data. Everything you create stays securely on your device.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Innovexa Technologies ("we", "us", or "our") manages user privacy in relation to our mobile application Taskly ("the Application"). We are fully committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have complete control over your personal data.

2. Data Collection and Usage

Taskly operates on a strict local-first model:

  • Task Data: Any tasks, titles, descriptions, due dates, statuses, and categories you create are saved exclusively in your device's local storage (via SharedPreferences / local SQLite).
  • Personal Information: We do NOT ask for or collect personal identifiers such as your name, phone number, email address, physical address, or contacts.
  • Account Creation: You do NOT need to create an account or sign in to use Taskly.
  • Sensors & Media: We do NOT access your camera, microphone, photo gallery, location, or sensitive sensors.

3. Third-Party Services & Analytics

Taskly does NOT integrate third-party ad networks, telemetry trackers, or external analytics SDKs (such as Google Analytics for Firebase, Facebook SDK, or AdMob). Your usage patterns and tasks remain completely private to you and never leave your mobile device.

4. Device Permissions

Taskly requires minimal or zero special device permissions to function. If notification permissions are requested in future updates, they will solely be used for scheduling local on-device task reminders that you set yourself.

5. Data Storage and Deletion

Because all data is stored locally on your device:

  • You can edit or delete any task at any time within the application.
  • Clearing the app storage/cache from your Android device settings will permanently remove all stored tasks.
  • Uninstalling the Application will remove all local data associated with Taskly.
  • Since we do not operate remote servers for your tasks, we do not retain any copies of your data.

6. Children’s Privacy

Our Application is safe for all audiences and does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under the age of 16 depending on local jurisdiction). If you are a parent or guardian and have questions regarding children's privacy, please contact us.

7. Compliance with Google Play Policies

This Privacy Policy complies with Google Play's User Data Policy. Taskly accurately represents its data practices: no personal data is collected, no data is shared with third parties, and no data is transferred across borders.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates or legal requirements. Any modifications will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
