NoteSphere Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 7, 2026
App: NoteSphere
Developer: Innovexa Technologies

This Privacy Policy explains how NoteSphere handles information when you use the app. It describes the app as currently implemented. If the app's features, dependencies, or data practices change, this policy must be updated before the updated app is released.

Information stored by the app

NoteSphere lets you create notes and manage category names. The app stores the following information on your device so its features work:

  • Note titles and note text.
  • Category names.
  • Notes and categories moved to the in-app Trash, until restored or permanently deleted.
  • App preferences, such as your selected light/dark mode and theme color, and a first-launch setting.

The current app stores this information using Android's local SharedPreferences storage. The inspected app code does not send this content to a NoteSphere developer-operated server, and the app does not require an account or provide cloud synchronization.

How information is used

Locally stored information is used to display and manage your notes, search your notes, manage category names and Trash, and remember your preferences. Note search runs within the app on your device.

Sharing, advertising, and analytics

The current app code does not integrate advertising, analytics, or social sharing features, and does not send note content or category names to the developer. This statement must be reviewed again if SDKs, services, or features are added in a future release.

Google Play and Android may process information in connection with installing, updating, securing, backing up, or operating your device. Those services are governed by Google's and your device provider's own terms and privacy information, not this policy.

Retention and deletion

Your notes, category names, and preferences remain in the app's local storage until you remove them or the app's local data is cleared. Items in Trash remain stored until restored or permanently deleted from Trash. Uninstalling the app normally removes its local app data from the device.

Android device backup settings or other device-level backup services may affect whether app data is backed up or restored. NoteSphere does not itself provide or manage a cloud backup service. Check your device's backup settings if you want to control device-level backups.

Security

The app stores its data locally using the platform's preferences storage. The current app does not implement a separate encryption or password-lock feature. Protect access to your device and avoid using the app to store information that requires a specific security or encryption guarantee.

Children's privacy

NoteSphere is a general-purpose notes app and is not specifically designed for children. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children through a developer-operated service. If the app's intended audience or data practices change, this section and the Play Console declarations must be reviewed.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app or its data practices change. The current version and its effective date will be published at the privacy policy URL provided on the Google Play Store listing.
