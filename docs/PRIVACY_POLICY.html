Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 9, 2026 | App: Noor ul Quran
Summary: Noor ul Quran is designed with full user privacy in mind. We do not require account registration, we do not track your activity, and we do not sell or transmit your personal data to external servers. All personal progress, bookmarks, and settings remain stored locally on your device.

1. Information Collection & Use

Noor ul Quran does not create, maintain, or require user accounts. We do not collect personal identification information such as your real name, email address, phone number, or physical address.

  • Profile Information: Any name or profile photo you set inside the app is saved locally on your device in secure storage (SharedPreferences) and is never uploaded.
  • Reading Progress & Notes: Your reading history, daily goals, memorized verses, bookmarks, and personal reflections are stored strictly in a local database (SQLite) on your device.

2. Internet & Network Usage

The app connects to the internet strictly to fetch Quranic text, translations, and recitation audio from established open APIs and Content Delivery Networks (CDNs):

  • Quran text & translations: api.alquran.cloud
  • Audio recitations: cdn.islamic.network, api.quran.com, mp3quran.net, quranicaudio.com

These requests do not carry any personal identifiers or account tokens. Standard web server logs (IP address and requested audio file) may be processed by these CDN providers solely to deliver media streams.

3. Device Permissions

Noor ul Quran requests only essential Android permissions required for media playback and optional reminders:

  • Internet Access (INTERNET): Needed to stream audio recitations and fetch Quran text.
  • Foreground Service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK): Keeps recitation playing smoothly when the app is in the background or screen is locked.
  • Wake Lock (WAKE_LOCK): Prevents audio stuttering during background Quran listening.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS - Optional): Used solely to trigger local scheduled daily reading reminders on your phone. No notifications are sent from external ad or marketing servers.
  • Storage / Camera Access: Used strictly when you explicitly choose a photo from your gallery or camera to use as your local profile picture.

4. Data Security & Storage

Because all user data resides locally on your device, your data security is tied to your physical device security. Uninstalling the app completely removes all locally stored data, including bookmarks and audio downloads.

5. Advertising & Analytics

Noor ul Quran is completely ad-free and tracker-free:

  • No third-party ad networks (e.g., Google AdMob, Unity Ads) are integrated.
  • No analytics tools (e.g., Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, AppsFlyer) are included.
  • No crash logging tools send data off your device.

6. Children's Privacy

Our application is suitable for all ages, including children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
