Privacy Policy

App Name: Calculator Fusion  |  Effective Date: November 14, 2026

1. Information stored by the app

The app does not require an account. It stores information you create in local app preferences on your device. This can include calculator expressions, results and history; saved calculation titles, expressions, results, calculator mode, saved time and optional notes; random-generator settings and history; theme and calculator-mode preferences; and selected date-calculator values.

The app does not operate a user account or app backend for these features. The current app implementation does not include advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

2. Storage, backup and deletion

This information is stored locally by the app. Secure Wallet entries are not encrypted and are not protected by a PIN or biometric lock. Do not save information there that requires cryptographic protection.

Android device backup may include app preferences depending on the Android version, device and account settings. Backups may be restored by the device or platform; this behavior is not controlled by the app.

You can clear calculator history and random-generator history using their in-app controls and delete saved calculations from Secure Wallet. Other preferences remain until changed or app data is cleared. Uninstalling generally removes local app data, but a device backup may restore data later.

3. Clipboard

When you tap a copy control, the selected value is placed on the device clipboard. Clipboard contents may be available to the operating system or other apps according to your device settings.

4. Permissions and third parties

The app does not request runtime permissions for its calculator features. The current release does not include advertising or analytics services. Review the Play Store Data Safety information for the exact release and its included components.

5. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app or its data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when this text is revised.

6. Contact
