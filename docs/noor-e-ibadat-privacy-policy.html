Official Privacy Policy

Developer: Innovexa Technologies | Effective Date: November 20, 2026

Privacy Summary: Noor-e-Ibadat respects your privacy. We do not sell, store, or share your personal data. Location access is used exclusively on your device in real-time to compute the direction of the Holy Kaaba (Qibla). No personal information ever leaves your device.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Noor-e-Ibadat ("we", "our", or "the App"), developed and owned by Innovexa Technologies. We are committed to protecting the privacy and personal data of our users.

This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles user information, the device permissions requested, and our data protection standards in compliance with the Google Play Developer Distribution Agreement and applicable privacy laws (such as GDPR and COPPA).

2. Information Collection and Usage

We believe in data minimization. The App is designed to function with maximum privacy, requiring no account creation, sign-up, or submission of personal identity.

A. Location Data (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION & ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION)

The App requests access to your device's approximate and precise geographic location. Here is exactly how it is handled:

  • Purpose: Your coordinates (latitude and longitude) are required strictly to calculate the precise geometric bearing (direction) towards the Holy Kaaba in Makkah (Qibla) from your current position on Earth.
  • Local Processing Only: All calculations are executed directly on your smartphone using mathematical algorithms.
  • No Transmission: Your location coordinates are NEVER sent to our servers, stored in any external database, or transmitted to any third parties.
  • No Background Tracking: Location is queried only while you are actively viewing the Qibla screen. The app does not track your movements or monitor background location.

B. Device Sensor Data (Compass, Magnetometer, Gyroscope)

To accurately display the Qibla needle heading relative to true north, the App accesses the real-time magnetic field and rotation sensors of your device. This sensor stream is processed strictly in volatile memory (RAM) in real time and is never recorded or stored.

C. Local App Storage & Preferences

When you use the Digital Tasbeeh counter, your count values, preset dhikr targets, and tactile vibration settings are saved locally on your device storage (via Android SharedPreferences). This data remains exclusively on your phone and can be reset or cleared at any time.

3. Third-Party Services and Analytics

Noor-e-Ibadat does not integrate third-party advertising SDKs, behavioral analytics trackers, or user profiling tools. The app uses standard, open-source Flutter development plugins:

  • flutter_qiblah: For communicating with device compass sensors and performing astronomical trigonometry.
  • permission_handler: For requesting runtime permissions according to Android security guidelines.

Neither of these libraries transmits your personal data or device identity to external servers.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our application is suitable for users of all ages, including families and children. Because we do not collect any personal information (names, emails, addresses, device identifiers, or biometric data), the App complies fully with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families Policy.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Because we do not collect, store, or transmit personal data to remote servers:

  • We do not retain any user records on external databases.
  • All local preferences and Tasbeeh counters are stored solely within your device's application sandbox.
  • To permanently erase all local data, you may simply tap Clear Data in your Android Settings (under Apps > Noor-e-Ibadat) or uninstall the application.

6. User Controls and Permissions

You maintain complete control over device permissions. You can grant or revoke location permissions at any time via your device settings (Settings > Apps > Noor-e-Ibadat > Permissions). Please note that while the Tasbeeh counter will continue to function normally without location permissions, the Qibla compass requires location access to calculate the Kaaba azimuth accurately.

7. Security of Your Information

We place the highest value on user trust. Because the application does not transmit personal data over the internet, your information is naturally protected against unauthorized interception, server breaches, and online leakage.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational, legal, or regulatory requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date". We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
