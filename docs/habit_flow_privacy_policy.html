Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Habit Flow is designed to work offline. Your habits, reminder schedules, app settings, and completion history are stored locally on your device and are not sent to a Habit Flow server.

This Privacy Policy explains how Habit Flow ("the app") handles information when you create, manage, and track habits.

Information the app uses

  • Habit information: habit names, descriptions or notes, schedules, and other details you choose to enter.
  • Completion history: the habits you mark as complete and the dates or timestamps associated with that activity.
  • Reminder settings: reminder times and notification preferences configured for your habits.
  • App settings: preferences such as theme and other settings used to operate the app.

How information is stored and shared

Habit Flow is designed for offline use and does not require an account or cloud synchronization. Habit information, completion records, reminder schedules, and app settings are stored in the app's local device storage. The app does not send this information to a Habit Flow server.

Device backups or operating-system services may handle app data according to your device settings and the platform provider's own policies. Habit Flow does not control those services.

Notifications and reminders

Habit Flow can use your device's notification feature to deliver habit reminders that you schedule. Notifications are delivered by the operating system. You can allow, deny, or change notification permissions in your device settings. If notifications are disabled, restricted, or affected by device power-management settings, scheduled reminders may not appear as expected.

Your choices

You can create, edit, or delete habits and manage completion records using the controls in the app. You can change reminder schedules and app preferences in Settings. You can also manage notification permissions through your device settings. Removing the app or clearing its storage may delete locally stored information, subject to any device backup or restore settings.

Children's privacy

Habit Flow does not require users to create an account. Habit names, schedules, notes, and completion information that users choose to enter are stored locally on the device.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. The latest version and its update date will be published with the app.
