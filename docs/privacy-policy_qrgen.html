Privacy Policy

QR Genio
Operated by Innovexa Technologies
Last updated: November 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how QR Genio handles information when you use the app. QR Genio creates QR codes on your device from text or other content that you enter.

Information we collect

QR Genio does not send the content you enter, generated QR codes, or personal information to servers operated by us. The app does not require an account. The current app does not include advertising or analytics services.

The content you enter and the generated QR code are used in the app while you create and preview a code. The app also stores your light/dark theme preference locally on your device.

Clipboard, saving, and sharing

  • If you choose to copy content, that content is placed on your device's clipboard. Clipboard behavior and retention may be controlled by your device or operating system.
  • If you choose to save a QR code, the app requests gallery access as needed and saves the PNG image to your device's gallery. The image remains there until you remove it using your device.
  • If you choose to share a QR code, the image is passed to the sharing interface and app you select. Those apps and their providers may handle the shared content under their own privacy policies.

Permissions

Gallery access is requested only when you choose to save a QR code. You can deny this access; saving to the gallery will then be unavailable. Other app features can still be used.

Third-party services

The current app does not use a QR Genio account or backend service to process your QR content. Android, your device manufacturer, and any sharing or gallery apps you choose may process information under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not control those services.

Data retention and deletion

QR content is handled locally while you use the generator and is not retained on our servers. You can clear entered content in the app. Copied content can be managed through your device's clipboard controls. Saved PNG images can be deleted from your gallery, and locally stored app preferences can be removed through your device's app settings or by uninstalling the app.

Children's privacy

QR Genio is a general-purpose utility and is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe information has been provided to us in error, contact us using the details below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its practices change. The updated policy will be published at the same policy URL, with a revised “Last updated” date.
